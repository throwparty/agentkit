# T-009: session isolation with cgroup lifecycle

**Question.** Can lifecycle use `--new-session` with cgroup v2 freeze on systemd hosts and process-group signalling elsewhere?

**Method.** `adrs/2026-10-31-linux-bwrap-lifecycle/poc/lifecycle-cgroup.sh` on NixOS, kernel 7.2.12, systemd user manager, bwrap 0.12.0.

**Observation.** The harness ran under `systemd-run --user --scope --unit=lbx-<pid>` with `--new-session`. `systemctl --user freeze` stopped the loop at 12 bytes; `systemctl --user thaw` resumed it to 18. `unfreeze` is not a systemd verb; `thaw` is. A frozen unit cannot be stopped, so cleanup thaws first. With `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=/nonexistent` the launcher exits 69 and the copied process-group `lifecycle.sh` freezes, resumes and kills.

**Conclusion.** Yes on systemd hosts. The fallback keeps process-group lifecycle and gives up session isolation.
