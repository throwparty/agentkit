# T-032: what happens to the kind in provenance?

**Question.** Once kinds leave the contract, what does the `kind` field in
per-step provenance hold?

**Method.** Read the provenance type and the contract type it names.

## Observation

`crates/agentkit-litterbox/src/sandbox/environment.rs:60-69`:

```rust
pub struct StepProvenance {
    pub identity: String,
    pub kind: ProvisionKind,
    pub variables: Vec<Variable>,
}
```

`ProvisionKind` is the closed enum at
`crates/agentkit-litterbox/src/sandbox/contract.rs:74`. T-027 moves kinds to a
registry outside the contract, so the contract can no longer name the type.

Provenance is rendered to users by `inspect` and the doctor. Dropping the field
would leave an identity with no statement of what kind of step produced the
change, which is the attribution those surfaces exist to give.

## Conclusion

Keep the field and change its type to the registry's kind name as a string. The
contract keeps identity and the kind name, which are facts a surface can print.
It does not keep a closed enum, so adding a kind does not change the contract.
